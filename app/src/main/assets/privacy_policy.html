Last updated: November 4, 2026

1Overview

Traffic Jam Rush is an offline, single-player puzzle game made by NG Studio ("we", "us"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the game uses, where it is kept, and the choices you have.

The short version: we don't ask you to create an account, and we don't collect your personal information. Your progress stays on your device.

2Information We Collect

  • No account, no personal info. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or any other personal details, and we do not collect them.
  • Game data on your device. Your level progress, stars, coins, boosters, unlocked collections, vehicle skins and settings (sound, music, vibration) are saved locally on your device so you can continue where you left off. This data is not sent to us.
  • No sensitive permissions. The game does not access your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

3Advertising

The game may show ads, including optional rewarded ads, through third-party ad networks such as Google AdMob, now or in a future update.

These networks may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device type and ad interaction data to show ads, limit how often you see them, and measure their performance. They handle this data under their own privacy policies. For Google, see:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time in your device settings (on Android: Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).

4Analytics & Crash Reports

The game currently does not include any analytics or crash-reporting tools.

If we add them in the future to fix bugs and improve levels, the data will be anonymized, and we will update this policy before that happens.

5Children's Privacy

Traffic Jam Rush is made for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

In line with COPPA (US) and GDPR-K (EU) rules for children's data, if ads are shown we aim to configure them appropriately for younger players. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has shared personal information with us, please contact us and we will delete it.

6Data Storage & Retention

All game data is stored only on your device, for as long as the game is installed.

  • To erase it, use Settings → Reset Progress in the game.
  • Uninstalling the game also deletes all of its local data.

7Your Rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR (EU/UK) and the CCPA/CPRA (California) give you rights over your personal data, including the right to:

  • know what data is collected and how it is used;
  • access, correct or delete your data;
  • object to or restrict processing, including for personalized ads;
  • not be discriminated against for using these rights.

Because we don't collect personal data ourselves, most requests can be handled directly on your device (Reset Progress, ad settings). For data held by ad networks, please use their tools, or contact us and we will help.

We do not sell your personal information.

8Security

Your game data stays in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. No method of storage is 100% secure, but keeping data local and minimal is the best protection we can offer.

9Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when we add new features or ad partners. The "Last updated" date at the top will always show the latest version. Continuing to play after an update means you accept the revised policy.

10Contact Us

Questions or requests about this policy? We're happy to help.
