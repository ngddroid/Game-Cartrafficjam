## Full description (1877/4000)
```
Traffic Jam: Car Escape Puzzle is a relaxing car parking puzzle. Tap a car and it drives off the way its arrow points, but only if the road is clear. Plan your moves, clear the traffic jam and get every car out of the lot!

🚗 HOW TO PLAY
• Every vehicle drives in the direction of its arrow
• Tap a car with a clear road and it zooms away
• Blocked cars bump into each other, so think before you tap
• Park cars and buses to pick up the passengers of the same color
• Clear the whole lot to win the level and earn stars

🧩 4 WAYS TO PLAY
• Classic – Unblock the parking lot and match every rider to a car of their color
• Crowd Loop – A giant crowd circles the ring. Park big buses and fill them up
• Pixel Crowd – The picture is made of people. Send buses and peel it layer by layer
• Sand Sweep – Free the trucks so they ride the belt and scoop up the sand picture

⭐ FEATURES
• 5,000 levels in every mode – 20,000 car puzzles to solve
• 50 colorful worlds to explore on the level map
• Difficulty that grows step by step, with Hard and Super Hard levels to master
• Handy boosters: Shuffle, Crane and Sort when you get stuck
• Collect coins, win daily rewards and keep your streak going
• Unlock fresh car skins and colors in the Garage
• Cute 3D toy cars, buses and trucks with smooth animations
• No timers and no pressure – play at your own pace
• Play offline, anytime, anywhere

🧠 TRAIN YOUR BRAIN
Every jam is a small logic puzzle. Plan the right order of moves, think a few steps ahead and watch the cars escape one by one. Easy to learn, satisfying to master and perfect for a quick break.

If you enjoy parking jam games, car escape puzzles, unblock games or sorting puzzles, you'll love clearing the lot in Traffic Jam: Car Escape Puzzle.

Can you clear every jam? Download now and start driving!

Questions or feedback? Write to us at store.ngdtechnolab@gmail.com
```
